# Sheetmanagement wiki

Sheetmanagement is a free web app for orchestras and ensembles: the **sheet music library**, **rehearsals**, **concerts** and **programmes** in one place. It sits on top of what many ensembles already use:

- **Google Drive**: one folder per piece, one PDF per part
- **Google Calendar**: rehearsals and concerts
- **Google Sheets**: which pieces were played when and where, plus members and rights

Drive and Calendar stay fully usable on their own. The app reads them and adds one combined view, made for members on their phones.
